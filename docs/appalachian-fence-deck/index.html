What we build

From a new privacy fence to an elevated deck off the back of the house, we handle the whole job: layout, site prep and installation.

Wood Fencing

Warm, natural privacy fences and custom layouts in pressure-treated lumber.

Vinyl Fencing

Clean lines and low upkeep: no staining, no painting, easy to wash.

Chain Link Fencing

An affordable, practical choice for pets, kids and larger yards.

Aluminum Fencing

Open, rust-resistant fencing well suited to pools and sloped lots.

Deck Construction

Ground-level to elevated decks with proper footings, tight framing, well-planned stairs and safe railings.

Fence & Deck Repair

Leaning posts, damaged boards, tired decks. We'll tell you plainly whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

Why homeowners choose us

  • Built for the terrain. We look at grade, drainage and soil before we dig, which helps prevent settling and leaning down the road.
  • Designed for how you live. Decks are laid out around traffic flow and furniture. Fences go where gates and access make sense.
  • Clear, upfront estimates. Pricing is based on your actual layout and materials, given after we walk the property.
  • Simple, direct communication. You'll know what's happening from the first call to the final walkthrough.
